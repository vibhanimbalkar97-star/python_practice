### Two important interview reminders

1\. `True is 1`

```
print(True == 1)  # Trueprint(True is 1)  # False
```

`==` compares values. `is` checks whether they are the same object. Python may show a `SyntaxWarning` for `is` with a literal like `1`; use `is` for identity checks, especially `is None`.

2\. Negative floor division

```
print(-10 // 3)  # -4print(-10 % 3)   # 2
```

Floor division rounds down toward negative infinity, and the remainder follows the sign of the divisor.
